About

Nehir Kaya
Hello, I am Nehir Kaya, a fictional demo photographer working in Gaziantep. For thirteen years I have shot portraits, street, architecture and product photography. I like simple composition, natural light and quiet moments. The person, address and prices on this site are fictional; the photographs are generated.
My approach
Restrained retouching, natural light and a mostly monochrome visual language.
How I work
I talk first, plan next and shoot last; every session ends with an online gallery.
