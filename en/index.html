Approach

Three principles

All photographs are fictional and generated; none are another photographer’s work.

  1. 01

    Natural light

    Flash is my last resort; windows, dawn and blue hour are my main light sources.

  2. 02

    Restrained retouching

    Skin, texture and colour stay natural; retouching only removes distractions.

  3. 03

    Quiet moments

    Waiting instead of posing; the frame is taken when the person forgets the camera.
